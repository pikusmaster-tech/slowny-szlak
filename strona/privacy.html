What data the game itself collects

The game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number. Your progress (level, coins, settings) is stored only on your device and is not sent to our servers. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from the Google AdMob network. To show ads and measure their performance, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device information and ad interactions. Google processes this data under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom see a consent form on first launch, where they can choose personalised or non-personalised ads. You can change this choice in the game: Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or turn off the advertising ID in your Android system settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to object, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office). Because the game does not store your data outside your device, Google is the quickest help with ad data. You can also write to the address above.
